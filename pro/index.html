Helpdesk Hero Pro

Your brand, your team, your reports

Pro is an add-on for the hub. It adds analytics and client reports, white label for every customer's help center, supporters with their own names, and asks customers how you did. Help Scout and Zendesk are coming soon.

Try the live demoSee pricing

What Pro adds

Analytics

Know what's happening, per site

Any date range from a week to a year, filtered by site: tickets over time, categories, priorities, tags, recurring issues, busiest sites and ratings.

  • Export PDF for a clean monthly report per client
  • Every chart has a table view
White label

Your name on every customer's dashboard

Rename the customer's help center ("Acme Care") and give it your logo, colour, link, contact details and opening hours, with a live preview. It updates on every connected site within minutes, down to the admin bar and footer.

Ratings and reviews

Ask customers how you did

When a ticket closes or access ends, customers rate support from one to five stars with a short comment. See it on the ticket, and the trend in Analytics.

AI triage

A brief on each ticket (likely cause, suspects, first steps) and reply drafts, with any AI provider in WordPress, including the free, private AI Provider for WebLLM that runs in your browser.

Supporters and agents

Choose who replies to customers and logs in to their sites, and the name customers see. A Support Agent role keeps them out of your site's admin.

Help Scout and Zendesk · coming soon

Tickets in your help desk with diagnostics attached, and replies syncing both ways.

Message every site

One dashboard notice to all your customers, for security releases or maintenance windows.

Pro questions

Do my customers need Pro?

No. Pro goes on your hub only. White label, ratings and messages reach customer sites through the free customer plugin.

What happens if my license ends?

Pro features switch off; tickets, sites, policies and the free hub keep working.

How do I install Pro?

Upload helpdesk-hero-pro.zip on your hub site under Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, then enter your license key under Support Hub → Pro. Full guide.

Can I try it first?

Yes: the live demo has every Pro feature switched on. On your own site, enter TEST as the license key.
