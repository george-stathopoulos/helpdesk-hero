The problem

Every WordPress ticket starts the same way

"Which version are you on? Which plugins? Can you send a screenshot? Can I have a login?" Three emails later, you can start.

Questions before answers

Versions, plugins, errors and "what changed?" take a day of emails before anyone can look at the actual problem.

Passwords in email

Shared admin passwords get forwarded, reused and forgotten. Nobody knows when access should end.

Customers in the dark

Once support logs in, the customer has no idea what was changed, and trust suffers when something breaks later.

How it works

One hub for your team. A help center on every customer site.

Install the free hub on your site, and give each customer a connection code for the free customer plugin.

  1. You send a code

    Create a connection code in the hub and choose the rules for that customer.

  2. They open a ticket

    From their dashboard, with versions, plugins, errors and recent changes attached, and a health check.

    DIAGNOSTICS

  3. You log in

    One click, a one-time link, a temporary account that ends on its own. No passwords.

    ACCESS

  4. You fix and reply

    From the hub. The customer sees your reply in their dashboard and by email.

  5. They see everything

    Every page you opened and setting you changed is listed for the customer.

Features

Everything a support team needs. Free.

No ticket limits, no site limits, no locked features. Pro adds integrations and reporting for teams who want them.

Tickets with answers attached

Start with the cause, not with questions

Every ticket arrives with the site's WordPress, PHP and server details, plugins and theme with versions, recent PHP and JavaScript errors, and what changed recently.

  • A health check flags conflicts, duplicate plugins and errors that started after an update
  • Recurring issues across all your customers, on one chart
  • Passwords and keys removed before anything leaves the site
Access without passwords

One click, and you're in

When the customer allows it, their site creates a temporary account for your team. Each login is a fresh one-time link. Access ends on its own, or when the ticket closes.

  • A safe default role: an administrator who can't manage users or edit code
  • Ask for more time; the customer approves, or it's automatic
  • Troubleshooting mode: switch plugins off for your session only
Your rules

Support policies and templates

Decide what access your team gets, for how long, what customers may change and what a ticket must include. Save policies as templates and apply them to one site or a hundred.

  • Standard, Hands-off, Strict and Full service templates to start from
  • Enforced on both sides: customers see only the options you allow
  • Change a template and every site using it updates

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is it for?

Anyone who supports other people's WordPress sites: agencies and freelancers with care plans, plugin and theme vendors, hosts and in-house teams.

What do my customers install?

The free Helpdesk Hero plugin. They paste the connection code you send them, and their help center follows your rules. A guide you can send them.

Can customers connect to my hub without permission?

No. Each connection code is created by you, works once and expires after 7 days. You can disconnect a site at any time.

Is there a server or account in the middle?

No. Your hub and your customers' sites talk directly, with signed requests. We never see your tickets.

Do I need Help Scout or Zendesk?

No. The hub has its own inbox and emails your team. Help Scout and Zendesk connections are coming soon in Pro.

Is it safe to give support access this way?

Safer than a shared password: no password exists, each link works once, access ends on its own, the default role can't manage users or edit code, and everything is logged for the customer. Security details.
